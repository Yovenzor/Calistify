Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

Calistify ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the Calistify app ("the Service").

By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

Account Data: Email address, username, and authentication tokens when you create an account.

Workout Data: Exercise logs, sets, reps, weights, durations, personal records, routines, and notes you create.

Profile Data: Bodyweight, training goals, and preferences you provide.

Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage, device type, operating system, and crash reports.

3. How We Use Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service, including storing your workout history
  • Calculate progress metrics such as training volume, personal records, and streaks
  • Display charts, analytics, and skill progression tracking
  • Sync your data across devices when you sign in
  • Send service-related notifications such as workout reminders (if enabled)
  • Improve the Service through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • Provide customer support when you contact us

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, and crash reporting services that help us operate the Service. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • App Stores: Apple App Store and Google Play for billing and subscription management.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

We never share your workout data with advertisers.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. Workout data is also cached locally on your device for offline access. When you sign in, your data syncs between your device and our servers.

If you use the Service without creating an account, your workout data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your workout data in a standard format
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@calistify.app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from our servers within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it longer.

Workout data stored locally on your device remains until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

8. Children's Privacy

Calistify is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens are securely stored. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy within the Service. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@calistify.app
