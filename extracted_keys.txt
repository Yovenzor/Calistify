guide.s3.intro = Logging a workout in Calistify is fast and distraction-free. The interface is designed so you can record your sets between exercises without losing your rhythm.

guide.s3.f4Text = After completing a set, a rest timer automatically counts down. Choose from quick presets or let the default run.

guide.s7.f2Text = Your saved routines live in the app. When it's time to train, select a routine and tap start — all your exercises are pre-loaded.

guide.s7.f3Text = Need to swap an exercise or add an extra set mid-workout? Edit any routine in real time without losing your logged data.

guide.s8.title = Rest Timer
guide.s8.intro = Built right into the workout interface, the rest timer keeps you on track between sets without needing a separate stopwatch or phone timer.
guide.s8.f1Title = Auto-Starting
guide.s8.f1Text = When you complete a set, the rest timer starts automatically with your default duration — no extra taps required.
guide.s8.f2Title = Quick Presets
guide.s8.f2Text = Choose from 30s, 60s, 90s, 2m, or 3m with a single tap. The visual ring counts down so you know exactly how much rest is left.
guide.s8.f3Title = Pause and Resume
guide.s8.f3Text = Need more rest? Pause the timer and resume when you're ready. You're always in control of your pacing.
guide.s8.f4Title = Motivational Alerts
guide.s8.f4Text = When rest ends, a sharp message gets you back to work. No lingering, no wasted time between sets.
guide.s8.tipTitle = PRO TIP
guide.s8.tipText = Stick to consistent rest intervals. For strength work, 2–3 minutes between sets is ideal. For hypertrophy, 60–90 seconds. Use the presets to match your training goal.
guide.s9.title = Pro Tips
guide.s9.intro = Get the most out of Calistify with these tried-and-true practices from experienced calisthenics athletes.
guide.s9.step1Title = Log Every Set
guide.s9.step1Text = The more data you log, the more accurate your progress charts and PRs become. Don't skip logging — even on lighter sessions.
guide.s9.step2Title = Use Routines for Consistency
guide.s9.step2Text = Build your go-to routines once and reuse them. This reduces setup time and keeps your training structured week after week.
guide.s9.step3Title = Update Your Bodyweight
guide.s9.step3Text = Bodyweight is part of your volume calculation. If your weight changes, update it in your profile so your analytics stay accurate.
guide.s9.step4Title = Focus on One Skill at a Time
guide.s9.step4Text = Skill progressions require patience. Pick one skill tree to focus on and work it 2–3 times per week until you advance a stage.
guide.s9.step5Title = Review Your Progress Weekly
guide.s9.step5Text = Take 5 minutes each week to review your charts. Spotting trends early helps you adjust before you plateau.
guide.s9.step6Title = Chase Small PRs
guide.s9.step6Text = Adding 1 kg or 1 rep is still progress. Small, consistent PRs compound into massive strength gains over months and years.
guide.s9.tipTitle = FINAL THOUGHT
guide.s9.tipText = The athletes who progress fastest aren't the ones who train hardest for a week — they're the ones who show up consistently for months. Calistify helps you build that habit by making tracking effortless.
