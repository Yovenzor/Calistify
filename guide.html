GETTING STARTED.

Welcome to Calistify — the calisthenics app built to track every rep, measure your progress, and help you master the skills you've been chasing. Here's how to get up and running in minutes.

1

Download the App

Calistify is available for Android. Download the APK file from our website and install it on your device. Make sure you've enabled installations from unknown sources in your phone's settings.

2

Create Your Profile

Enter your bodyweight and training goals. This helps Calistify calculate accurate training volume and suggest appropriate progression levels for your skill work.

3

Start Your First Workout

Tap "Start Workout" on your dashboard, pick the exercises you want to train, and begin logging your sets. It's that simple — no complicated setup required.

Pick Your Pull-Up Bar and Go

Calistify works with any calisthenics setup — from a single pull-up bar in the park to a full outdoor calisthenics park. All you need is your bodyweight and a place to train.

2

YOUR DASHBOARD.

The dashboard is your training home base. It gives you a quick snapshot of where you are and what to do next, so you can walk into every session with a plan.

Streak Counter

See your current training streak at a glance. Stay consistent and watch the number climb — it's a powerful motivator.

Today's Workout

Your next planned session appears front and center. Tap "Start Workout" to jump straight into your training.

Quick Stats

Your latest PR and total reps are displayed in compact cards, so you always know where you stand.

Weekly Activity

A simple bar chart shows which days you trained this week, giving you a visual snapshot of your consistency.

PRO TIP

Check your dashboard before every session. It shows you what's planned, where your streak stands, and what PRs you're chasing — so you never walk into a workout unprepared.

3

TRACKING WORKOUTS.

Logging a workout in Calistify is fast and distraction-free. The interface is designed so you can record your sets between exercises without losing your rhythm.

1

Start a Workout

Tap "Start Workout" from the dashboard. A live workout timer starts running immediately, so you can track the total duration of your session.

2

Add Exercises

Browse the exercise library and add movements to your session. You can mix bodyweight and weighted exercises freely — Calistify handles both.

3

Log Each Set

For each set, enter the weight (if any), reps, and any hold duration. Tap the checkmark to mark the set as complete. You can add as many sets as you need.

4

Use the Built-In Rest Timer

After completing a set, a rest timer automatically counts down. Choose from quick presets (30s, 60s, 90s, 2m, 3m) or let the default run.

Weighted or Bodyweight — Track It All

Whether you're doing bodyweight pull-ups or weighted dips with extra kilos, Calistify captures every detail. Volume is calculated automatically using your bodyweight plus any added weight.

PRO TIP

Log your sets immediately after completing them. This keeps your data accurate and means you never forget a rep or a weight. The rest timer gives you the perfect window to do this.

4

PROGRESS ANALYTICS.

Every set you log feeds into your progress analytics. Calistify turns raw training data into clean, visual charts so you can see exactly how your strength is growing over time.

Training Volume

See your total training volume over time. Volume is calculated as (bodyweight + added weight) × reps × sets, giving you a true measure of your workload.

Workout Frequency

Track how often you train. A calendar view shows your training days, making it easy to spot gaps and stay on schedule.

Exercise Progression

Drill into any exercise and see how your strength has improved on that specific movement — your top sets, average volume, and trend over weeks and months.

Estimated 1RM

Calistify estimates your one-rep max based on your top sets, so you can gauge your maximal strength without attempting a true max lift.

Watch the Trend Line Climb

Your progress charts update automatically after every workout. The upward trend line is one of the most satisfying sights in calisthenics — proof that your work is paying off.

5

PERSONAL RECORDS.

Every PR is celebrated. Calistify automatically detects when you've beaten a personal best and logs it — no manual tracking required.

1

Automatic PR Detection

When you log a set that exceeds your previous best for an exercise, Calistify flags it as a new personal record and updates your PR history.

2

View Your PR Collection

All your personal records are collected in one view, showing the value, the date achieved, and the change since your last best.

3

Track Monthly Changes

Each PR shows how much you've improved in the past month, so you can see whether you're plateauing or still climbing.

Every Kilogram Counts

Whether it's adding 5 kg to your weighted pull-up or squeezing out 3 more reps on bodyweight dips, Calistify makes sure no achievement goes unnoticed.

6

SKILL PROGRESSIONS.

From your first tuck hold to a full front lever, Calistify's skill trees show you exactly where you are and what comes next. No more guessing what progression to work on.

Visual Skill Trees

Each skill has a clear progression path — from beginner variations to the full hold. Mark each stage as you advance.

Hold Duration Tracking

For static skills like the front lever or L-sit, log your hold time for each progression stage and watch it grow session by session.

Six Core Skills

Track progress toward the handstand, front lever, planche, muscle-up, back lever, and human flag — the holy grails of calisthenics.

Know What's Next

The skill tree shows you which progression to focus on next, so you always have a clear training target.

From Tuck to Full

Skill mastery is a journey. Calistify breaks each skill into manageable stages — tuck, advanced tuck, one leg, straddle, full — so you always know what to train and how far you've come.

7

CUSTOM ROUTINES.

Build reusable workout templates tailored to your goals. Set up your exercises, target sets, and reps once — then launch the routine in seconds every time you train.

1

Create a Routine

Name your routine (e.g., "Pull Day" or "Push Day"), then add exercises from the library. Set target sets and reps for each movement.

2

Save and Reuse

Your saved routines live in the app. When it's time to train, simply select a routine and tap start — all your exercises and set targets are pre-loaded.

3

Modify On the Fly

Need to swap an exercise or add an extra set mid-workout? You can edit any routine in real time without losing your logged data.

Build Your Perfect Split

Whether you follow a push/pull split, an upper/lower split, or a full-body routine, Calistify adapts to your training style. Create as many routines as you need.

8

THE REST TIMER.

Built right into the workout interface, the rest timer keeps you on track between sets without needing a separate stopwatch or phone timer.

Auto-Starting

When you complete a set, the rest timer starts automatically with your default duration — no extra taps required.

Quick Presets

Choose from 30s, 60s, 90s, 2m, or 3m with a single tap. The visual ring counts down so you know exactly how much rest is left.

Pause and Resume

Need more rest? Pause the timer and resume when you're ready. You're always in control of your pacing.

Motivational Alerts

When rest ends, a sharp message gets you back to work. No lingering, no wasted time between sets.

PRO TIP

Stick to consistent rest intervals. For strength work, 2–3 minutes between sets is ideal. For hypertrophy, 60–90 seconds. Use the presets to match your training goal.

9

PRO TIPS.

Get the most out of Calistify with these tried-and-true practices from experienced calisthenics athletes.

1

Log Every Set, Every Time

The more data you log, the more accurate your progress charts and PRs become. Don't skip logging — even on lighter sessions.

2

Use Routines for Consistency

Build your go-to routines once and reuse them. This reduces setup time and keeps your training structured week after week.

3

Update Your Bodyweight Regularly

Bodyweight is part of your volume calculation. If your weight changes, update it in your profile so your analytics stay accurate.

4

Focus on One Skill at a Time

Skill progressions require patience. Pick one skill tree to focus on and work it 2–3 times per week until you advance a stage.

5

Review Your Progress Weekly

Take 5 minutes each week to review your charts. Spotting trends early helps you adjust your training before you plateau.

6

Chase Small PRs, Not Big Jumps

Adding 1 kg or 1 rep is still progress. Small, consistent PRs compound into massive strength gains over months and years.

Consistency Beats Intensity

The athletes who progress fastest aren't the ones who train hardest for a week — they're the ones who show up consistently for months. Calistify helps you build that habit by making tracking effortless.
